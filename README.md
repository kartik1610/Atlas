# Atlas — Travel Journal

A personal travel journal for your phone: a globe / flat world map with pins for every place you've been, memories as postcards, photos & videos, travel buddies, wishlist, state & district fills, yearly Wrapped and zip backups.

**Install:** open https://kartik1610.github.io/Atlas/ on your phone
- Android (Chrome): ⋮ → Install app
- iPhone (Safari): Share → Add to Home Screen

**Photo import:** tap 📷 to drop in photos — Atlas reads each photo's location and date and sorts them into the right trips.
**Faces:** turn on in ⚙ → Face recognition. It runs on your phone only; name someone once and they're tagged everywhere.

Everything you add stays on your own phone. Use ⚙ → Back up journal to save a copy.
